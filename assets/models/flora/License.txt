Vegetation and rocks used for the scenery.

st-oak-*, st-columnar-*, st-pine-*: Stylized Trees pack (maker unknown).
sn-*: Stylised Nature by Elijah Cobden (no licence file shipped with the pack).
flower-*, fern-1: Stylized Nature MegaKit by Quaternius, CC0 (public domain).

The models were resized, their textures reduced to 256 px and their leaves cut out for use in this game.
